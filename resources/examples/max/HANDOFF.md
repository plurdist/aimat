## New direction (decided 2026-10-08, in progress)
`aimat_relay_grains` didn't work. The clouds don't blend: its synchronous grain grid buzzes, and note-by-note gating makes them stutter. Passes also weren't tangible. The replacement is **`aimat_relay_clouds`**, which is not built yet:
- **Continuations are read, not played.** The listener sends `/phrase_profile <path> …` after each MIDI result (AIM2-65): chord, weights, register, range, density, legato, velocity, jagged, regularity and change. Those numbers set the voice for the whole turn.
- **Voice = Grainflow cloud + resonators.** Each voice is a `grainflow~` cloud (Grainflow 2.1.2 is installed in Max 9) through two `fffb~` resonator banks tuned to the phrase's chord. A pass changes the chord by crossfading between the banks over 4 s.
- **v1 pass gesture:** the receiver swells to the foreground. When a player presses PASS, their cloud thins and darkens at once, then settles over about 20 s to a quiet **floor** that holds their last chord. Speaker-to-speaker travel comes later.
- **PASS sends the held phrase straight to the Continuator** (AIM2-11, which lets models read files from any folder).
- **Built 2026-10-08, not yet tested in Max:** `aimat_relay_clouds.maxpat` plus the voice abstraction `chain_voice.maxpat`, both generated by `tools/make_relay_clouds.py`. Engines: `chain_voice.js` (spec `tools/test_voice.js`, 36 checks) and `chain_relay_clouds.js` (spec `tools/test_relay_clouds.js`, 29 checks). The old engines are unchanged, so the fallback patches still work.
- **Calibrated on real data.** The voice mappings are set from the profiles of the 953 MIDI files in `~/aimat` (median density 6–11 notes/s, velocity about 0.4). Each character folds its chord into its own register: drift 80–330 Hz, dust 180–720 Hz, glass 350–1400 Hz. Chord notes with weight below 0.35 are left out.

- **Effects loop (done):** the returns are spread L / mid / R across speakers 1/2/3, delay→reverb is taken straight from the plug-in (unaffected by solo), and the dry source is an optional "source bed" toggle that is off by default.

## The piece
A phrase is handed between three players through a chain of models. Musika generates a source, Basic Pitch transcribes it, and the transcription is generation 0. When a player presses PASS, the Continuator continues their phrase, mutated by that player's **twist**, and the result lands on the next player (1 → 2 → 3 → 1). Working title: *The Illusion* (the illusion of free will; Eric is writing the score himself). Eric dropped the BED; there is one source only.

## How `aimat_relay_grains` works
- **The grains:** note number sets where the grains read (glides over 300 ms), note on/off swells and fades the cloud (fade = **tail**), velocity sets level, and pitch is ignored.
- **The characters:** P1 **dust** (40 ms, scattered), P2 **drift** (400 ms, an octave down), P3 **glass** (120 ms, an octave up).
- **Per-player knobs:** size, spray, tail and pitch (±24 semitones, plus a "pitch in steps" switch for octaves and fifths), alongside PASS, twist and pace.
- **The grain engine:** four overlapping streams (`phasor~` → `wrap~` offsets → `sah~` position + `noise~` scatter → `clip~` → `wave~ relay_source`), each multiplied by a Hann window (`cos~`). Even streams go left, odd streams right.
- **Routing:** each speaker plays its player's voice plus the "room" (SOURCE, DDSP, REVERB, DELAY, folded to mono). The speaker output numbers are boxes on the screen. **Headphones mode** sends everything to outputs 1/2. Master level, soft limiter (`tanh~`) and STOP ALL (Esc) apply to every output.
- **MIDI:** all player controls are `live.*` objects, so Eric maps controllers in Max's MIDI Map mode, then saves.

## Lessons from this run
- Max only fills in `#1` at the **start** of a name (`#1_gate` works, `chain_gate_#1` doesn't).
- `svf~` is unstable above about a quarter of the sample rate. Use `filtercoeff~` + `biquad~` instead.
- An empty Basic Pitch transcription (for example from the techno model) used to cause a runaway loop of Continuator runs. The engines now detect empty phrases and rate-limit requests.
- The scratch folder gets cleared after a few days, so keep generators in `tools/`.
